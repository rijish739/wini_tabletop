## Mean of Ungrouped Data  (`grade9::mean_of_ungrouped_data`)
*Class-9 topic:* Statistics  |  *bridges to:* jemh113__mean_grouped_data

**Recap:** Class IX covered finding the mean as a measure of central tendency for ungrouped data. If observations x1, ..., xn have frequencies f1, ..., fn, the mean (x̄) is calculated as the sum of (fi * xi) divided by the sum of all frequencies (Σfi). This can be written as x̄ = Σ(f_i * x_i) / Σf_i.

**Diagnostic:** Given observations x1, x2, ..., xn with frequencies f1, f2, ..., fn, what is the formula for the mean of this ungrouped data?

**Expected answer:** x̄ = Σ(f_i * x_i) / Σf_i

**Recap:** A fundamental concept in geometry is that the shortest distance from an external point to a given line is the length of the perpendicular segment from the point to the line. **Diagnostic:** What is true about the line segment that represents the shortest distance from a point not on a line to that line?

**Expected answer:** It is perpendicular to the line.
